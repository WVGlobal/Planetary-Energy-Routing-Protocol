# 🌍 Start Here — WVGlobal Open‑Source Initiative

## What WVGlobal Is
WVGlobal is a decentralized, open‑source framework for planetary stabilization.  
It connects energy, materials, atmosphere, agriculture, and circular‑economy systems into one federated architecture — designed to help humanity balance the planet safely and transparently.

## Our Mission
To build a public, non‑corporate protocol that enables global climate resilience through open collaboration, ethical AI, and decentralized governance.

## The Five Pillars
| Pillar | Focus | Goal |
|--------|--------|------|
| **AGMS** | Autonomous Grid Management System | Federated AI mesh for global energy routing |
| **AREM** | Algorithmic Re‑Engineering of Materials | Carbon‑negative materials and open molecular databases |
| **Geoengineering** | Atmospheric Rebalancing | Safe, data‑driven SRM and ocean carbon absorption |
| **PALO** | Precision Agriculture & Land Optimization | Transition to biosynthetic proteins and ecological restoration |
| **ACE** | Automated Circular Economy | Closed‑loop manufacturing and autonomous reclamation |

## How to Get Involved
1. Read the overview in the repository.  
2. Join a pillar that matches your expertise or interest.  
3. Open an Issue to propose ideas or ask questions.  
4. Start a Discussion to collaborate on design or simulations.  
5. Submit a Pull Request when you have code, data, or documentation to contribute.
