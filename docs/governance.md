# Governance Framework

## Purpose

The Governance Framework ensures that PERP remains transparent, accountable, secure, and aligned with human interests.

## Guiding Principles

### 1. Human Oversight

Major decisions affecting critical infrastructure must remain subject to human review and intervention.

### 2. Transparency

Protocol operations should be observable and auditable wherever practical.

### 3. Local Sovereignty

Participation in PERP does not remove local ownership or decision-making authority.

Communities retain control of their assets and infrastructure.

### 4. Open Standards

Protocol specifications should remain publicly available and open for review.

### 5. Security

Strong cybersecurity practices are mandatory to protect participating systems.

### 6. Sustainability

Decision-making should support environmental and social sustainability objectives.

### 7. Fair Access

PERP should encourage equitable participation across communities and regions.

## Governance Structure

Potential future governance participants may include:

- Energy providers
- Researchers
- Governments
- Community organizations
- Technology partners
- Independent experts

## Auditing

All critical routing decisions should be traceable through verifiable audit logs.

## Emergency Authority

In emergency situations, authorized operators must retain the ability to override automated decisions where necessary.

## Long-Term Objective

Develop a governance model capable of supporting regional, national, and international energy coordination while maintaining trust and accountability.
