# Research Questions

The following questions guide ongoing development of the Planetary Energy Routing Protocol.

## Energy Routing

1. What routing methodologies best optimize distributed energy resources?

2. How can transmission losses be minimized?

3. How should competing energy demands be prioritized?

---

## Artificial Intelligence

4. Can federated AI improve coordination without centralizing control?

5. What level of autonomy is appropriate for infrastructure decisions?

6. How can AI recommendations remain explainable and auditable?

---

## Governance

7. What governance model best balances efficiency and accountability?

8. How should conflicts between participating regions be resolved?

9. What safeguards prevent misuse of the protocol?

---

## Security

10. What cybersecurity architecture is required for planetary-scale deployment?

11. How should identity and authentication be managed?

12. How can critical infrastructure be protected from malicious actors?

---

## Interoperability

13. How can different energy systems communicate using common standards?

14. Which existing protocols should PERP integrate with?

15. What technical barriers exist to large-scale adoption?

---

## Future Technologies

16. How might wireless power transmission integrate into PERP?

17. How should energy storage networks participate?

18. Could PERP support future planetary-scale energy infrastructure?

## Open Invitation

Researchers, engineers, policymakers, and innovators are encouraged to contribute additional questions and research directions.
