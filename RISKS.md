## ⚖️ Addressing Systemic Risks & Geopolitical Reality

The PERP architecture operates on a planetary scale, meaning technical design cannot be separated from geopolitical and ecological realities. We explicitly acknowledge the following design constraints:

### 1. The Geoengineering Containment Problem (Pillar 3)
We recognize that Solar Radiation Management (SRM) and Ocean Iron Fertilization carry severe risks of unintended regional climate feedback loops (e.g., monsoon disruption). PERP does not view geoengineering as a permanent solution, but as a temporary thermal buffer. 
*   **Our Mitigation:** This is why Pillar 3 hardcodes absolute veto parameters into the local AI nodes. If marine sensor telemetry flags a localized ecological anomaly, the protocol triggers an automated, irreversible intervention halt.

### 2. Multi-National Weaponization & Trust
Coordinating space-based solar meshes across competing national aerospace entities presents a massive political challenge. 
*   **Our Mitigation:** The Federated Agentic Mesh uses zero-knowledge data isolation. Nodes only communicate baseline generation and load capacity via the laser link, meaning no state or corporation ever shares proprietary military or commercial data to achieve global optimization.

### 3. The Reality of the Scope
PERP is an open-source protocol stack, not a centralized infrastructure company. Like the original foundations of the internet (TCP/IP), our goal is to design the shared multi-agent data contracts, leaving the physical execution to independent global builders.
