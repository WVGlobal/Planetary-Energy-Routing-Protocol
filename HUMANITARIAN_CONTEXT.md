# 🕊️ Humanitarian Integration: Socio-Technical Safeguards

The Planetary Energy Routing Protocol (PERP) is engineered as an open-source technical protocol, but its execution is inseparable from the preservation of human life and regional stability. 

Through **Pillar 6 (Local Sovereignty & Community Resilience)**, PERP actively aligns with global citizen-led humanitarian frameworks and international peacebuilding objectives.

## ⚖️ Interlocking Objectives

Our technical architecture is designed to provide the foundational data contracts that make human-centric safety and community preservation operationally possible in times of global stress:

*   **Protection of Civilians & Infrastructure:** While international humanitarian frameworks demand the physical safety of hospitals, schools, and aid corridors, **Pillar 1 and Pillar 6** provide the software protocols to guarantee baseline survival power, clean water tracking, and encrypted communication loops directly to those localized municipal assets during regional conflicts.
*   **Constructive, Non-Intrusive Utility:** The protocol treats technology as an adaptable tool. By enforcing strict **Encrypted Telemetry Isolation**, the mesh ensures that local community data, public health resource maps, and regional survival infrastructure can never be tracked, monitored, or weaponized by external conflicting forces.
*   **Common-Ground Technical Architecture:** Just as meaningful peace begins when parties are willing to engage on common ground, PERP provides a neutral, open-source, zero-knowledge data language where independent nations and microgrids can safely cooperate to balance planetary survival resources without needing political or ideological alignment.

We invite humanitarian designers, peacebuilding advocates, and international policy analysts to join the Wider View Global maintainer community to further refine these technical safeguards.
