# Vision

## Overview

The Planetary Energy Routing Protocol (PERP) is an open-source framework for coordinating energy generation, storage, transmission, and consumption across decentralized energy networks.

As the world transitions toward renewable energy systems, energy infrastructure is becoming increasingly distributed. Solar arrays, wind farms, battery storage systems, electric vehicles, microgrids, and future wireless power technologies create new opportunities for energy resilience and sustainability.

PERP explores how federated artificial intelligence and open communication standards can help coordinate these systems while preserving local autonomy, transparency, and human oversight.

## Mission

To create an open, interoperable protocol that enables energy systems to cooperate intelligently across communities, regions, nations, and eventually planetary-scale infrastructure.

## Long-Term Goal

A future in which energy can be routed, balanced, and shared as efficiently as information travels across the Internet today.

## Core Values

- Openness
- Transparency
- Sustainability
- Interoperability
- Human Governance
- Local Sovereignty
- Resilience

## Vision Statement

PERP seeks to become a foundational protocol for a future energy ecosystem in which decentralized resources work together to deliver reliable, sustainable, and equitable access to energy for all.
