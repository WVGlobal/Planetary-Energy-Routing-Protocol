# Contributing to PERP

Thank you for your interest in the Planetary Energy Routing Protocol (PERP).

PERP is an open-source initiative exploring how federated intelligence, open standards, and distributed energy systems can work together to improve the resilience, sustainability, and efficiency of future energy networks.

## Areas of Contribution

We welcome contributions in:

- Energy systems engineering
- Renewable energy
- Artificial intelligence
- Federated learning
- Grid operations
- Cybersecurity
- Governance frameworks
- Policy development
- Software architecture
- Sustainability research

## How to Contribute

### Report an Issue

If you identify a problem, inconsistency, or potential improvement, please open an issue.

### Suggest Enhancements

Ideas that improve the architecture, governance, security, or practicality of PERP are encouraged.

### Submit Documentation Improvements

Documentation is critical for protocol development.

Contributors are encouraged to improve:

- Technical specifications
- Use cases
- Research questions
- Governance proposals
- Visual diagrams

### Participate in Discussion

Constructive discussion helps refine the protocol and identify weaknesses before implementation.

## Core Principles

All contributions should support:

- Transparency
- Human oversight
- Local autonomy
- Open standards
- Sustainability
- Security
- Respectful collaboration

## Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to:

- Treat others respectfully
- Support evidence-based discussion
- Avoid personal attacks
- Focus on the long-term success of the project

## Future Contributors

PERP welcomes participation from:

- Researchers
- Engineers
- Energy providers
- Governments
- Community organizations
- Technology companies
- Students
- Independent innovators

Thank you for helping shape the future of intelligent energy coordination.
