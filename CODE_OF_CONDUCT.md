# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The PERP community is committed to creating an open, inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment.

We welcome contributors regardless of:

- Background
- Experience level
- Profession
- Nationality
- Beliefs

## Expected Behaviour

Participants should:

- Be respectful
- Be constructive
- Be evidence-based
- Support collaboration
- Encourage diverse viewpoints

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Intimidation
- Deliberate misinformation
- Disruptive behaviour

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or participation that violate these principles.

## Guiding Philosophy

PERP seeks to develop technologies that benefit humanity and the planet.

All contributors should act in a manner consistent with those goals.
